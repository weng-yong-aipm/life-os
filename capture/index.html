How this works

This page only ever writes. It queues the link and nothing more — it cannot read the queue back, and neither can anything else signed in as you. The Desk drains the queue outbound on its own machine, fetches the content, summarises it, and files it into the knowledge inbox.

That one-way shape is the point: the Desk keeps binding to 127.0.0.1 and gains no exposure from this page existing.

Tracking parameters (utm_*, fbclid, igshid…) are stripped before the link is stored.
